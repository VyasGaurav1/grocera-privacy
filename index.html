Privacy Policy – Grocera

Effective Date: October 17, 2026

Grocera is an offline grocery-list application designed to help individuals and households create and manage grocery shopping lists. It includes predefined grocery items in English and Hindi.

Your privacy is important to us. This policy explains how Grocera handles information when you use the app.

1. Information We Collect

Grocera does not collect or transmit users' personal information to us or any third party. The app does not require an account, registration, sign-in, or personal details.

We do not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, location data, contacts, advertising identifiers, or usage analytics through the app.

2. Data Stored on Your Device

Grocera may store grocery lists, item selections, language preferences, and app settings locally on your device, where applicable. This information is not transmitted to our servers or external services by Grocera.

Predefined grocery item names in English and Hindi are provided for the app's functionality. Clearing app data or uninstalling the app may permanently delete locally stored information.

3. Offline Functionality

Grocera is designed to work entirely offline. Its core grocery-list functionality does not require an internet connection, online accounts, or cloud storage.

4. Data Sharing and Third-Party Services

Grocera does not sell, rent, trade, or share users' personal information. The app does not use third-party advertising, analytics, or tracking services.

5. Data Security

Information managed by the app remains in the local device environment. Users are responsible for securing their devices. Locally stored information may be lost if the device is damaged, reset, or the app is removed.

6. Children's Privacy

Grocera does not knowingly collect personal information from children or other users. The app does not require users to submit personal information.

7. Your Privacy Choices

You can use Grocera without providing personal information or creating an account. You can manage locally stored information through the app's available features and your device settings.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This policy may be updated if the app's functionality or privacy practices change, or to comply with applicable requirements. The latest version will be published at this webpage.
